# Learning Log Feature

## 📚 Overview
The Learning Log is a simple and clear tracking system that monitors your Swedish vocabulary learning progress. It provides straightforward insights into which words you've encountered and how often you've practiced them.

## 🎯 Features

### 📊 Simple Progress Tracking
- **Word Statistics**: Track how many times you've reviewed each word
- **Review Count**: Clear display of practice frequency for each word
- **Date Tracking**: See when words were added and last reviewed

### 🔍 Search & Sort
- **Search**: Find specific words by Swedish or English text
- **Sort Options**: 
  - **Most Recent** - Recently reviewed words first
  - **Alphabetical** - Swedish words in alphabetical order
  - **Most Reviewed** - Most practiced words first
- **Smart Results**: Shows matching word count and total words

### 📈 Clear Analytics Dashboard
- **Total Words Learned**: Complete count of your vocabulary
- **Total Reviews**: Sum of all practice sessions
- **Average Reviews**: How often you typically practice words

### 🎵 Audio Support
- **Pronunciation**: Play Swedish pronunciation for any word
- **Consistent Experience**: Same audio feature as flashcards

## 🚀 How to Access

### From Main Screen
1. Click the **"Learning Log"** button in the top-right corner
2. On mobile, the button shows as **"Log"**

### Navigation
- **Back Arrow**: Return to flashcard practice
- **Responsive Design**: Works perfectly on all screen sizes
