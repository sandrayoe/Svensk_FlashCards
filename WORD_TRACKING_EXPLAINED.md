# How Word Tracking Works in Svenska FlashCards

## 📚 What Gets Counted?

### "Seen X times" means:
A word gets counted **each time it appears in a practice session**. Here's exactly when:

1. **Daily Word Generation**: When you first open the app each day and it generates 5 new Swedish words
2. **Regenerate Words**: When you click "Regenerate Words" to get a fresh set of 5 words

### What does NOT get counted:
- ❌ Flipping individual flashcards back and forth
- ❌ Playing the pronunciation audio
- ❌ Just viewing the Learning Log
- ❌ Navigation between screens

## 🎯 Example Scenarios

### Day 1:
- You open the app → Gets 5 words: "hund", "katt", "bil", "hus", "bok"
- Each word is now **"Seen 1 time"**

### Day 2: 
- You open the app → Gets 5 words: "katt", "bil", "träd", "vatten", "mat"
- "katt" and "bil" are now **"Seen 2 times"** (appeared on both days)
- "träd", "vatten", "mat" are **"Seen 1 time"** (new words)
- "hund", "hus", "bok" stay at **"Seen 1 time"** (didn't appear today)

### Same Day - Regenerate:
- You click "Regenerate Words" → Gets 5 new words: "bil", "mat", "gul", "stor", "ny"
- "bil" and "mat" increment to **"Seen 3 times"** and **"Seen 2 times"** respectively
- "gul", "stor", "ny" start at **"Seen 1 time"**

## 📊 Statistics Meaning

### In the Learning Log:
- **Total Words Learned**: Unique Swedish words you've encountered
- **Total Sessions**: Sum of all word appearances across all practice sessions  
- **Average Sessions**: How often words typically appear (Total Sessions ÷ Total Words)

### For Each Word:
- **"Seen X times"**: Number of practice sessions this word appeared in
- **"Added [date]"**: First time you encountered this word
- **"Last seen [date]"**: Most recent practice session with this word

## 🎯 Why This System?

This tracking method focuses on **word exposure frequency** rather than individual interactions. It helps you see:

- Which words the AI gives you most often (probably common/useful words)
- Your overall vocabulary growth over time
- Words you haven't seen in a while (might need refreshing)

## 💡 Tips for Better Learning

1. **Regular Practice**: Use the app daily to see natural word repetition patterns
2. **Regenerate Strategically**: If you want to practice specific words more, regenerate until they appear
3. **Check Your Log**: See which words appear frequently vs. rarely
4. **Long-term View**: Track your vocabulary growth over weeks and months

The system is designed to be simple and reflect your actual learning exposure to Swedish vocabulary! 🇸🇪
