I use starter from Firebase Studio, Next.js framework.

About the app:
Simple flashcards generator for your daily learning of Swedish words. 5 words per click/session. You can regenerate if you are unhappy/already know the words. 
 
I connect with Gemini AI to generate words.

Difficulty: B1 to C1 in professional settings. 

Screen(s):
One for the flashcards and another one for your learning logs.
